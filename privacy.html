Privacy Policy — Studio Publisher

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Studio Publisher is a personal content-scheduling tool that connects to your own TikTok account through TikTok's official login (OAuth). This policy explains what data we handle and why.

1. Data we collect and why

DataWhy we need it
TikTok account identifier (open_id) and basic profile info (scope: user.info.basic)To know which of your accounts a queued post belongs to.
OAuth access and refresh tokens (scopes: video.upload, video.publish)To upload and publish only the videos you approve, at the times you schedule. Nothing else.
Video files, captions, hashtags, and posting settings you provideTo publish exactly the posts you approved, with the settings you chose (e.g. AI-generated label, public visibility).

2. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data with any third party.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or analytics.
  • We do not access your TikTok direct messages, followers list, or any data outside the permissions listed above.

3. Storage and security

Tokens are stored securely on the owner's own server with restricted file permissions, and are used only to perform the posting actions you approved. Tokens are never logged, never embedded in URLs, and never sent anywhere except TikTok's official API.

4. Retention

We keep your tokens and queued post data only for as long as you use the tool. Queued videos and post records can be deleted by you at any time.

5. Your rights

  • Revoke anytime: disconnect the app in TikTok's Settings > Security > Manage app permissions — this immediately invalidates our stored tokens.
  • Delete: ask us to delete all stored data for your account and we will do so promptly.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date above will reflect changes.
