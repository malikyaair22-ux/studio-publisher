Studio Publisher

A personal tool to schedule and publish your own TikTok videos — nothing posts without your per-post approval.

How it works

  1. Connect your TikTok account through TikTok's official login. You approve exactly what the app may do, and you can revoke access anytime from TikTok Settings.
  2. Queue a video with its caption, hashtags, and posting time.
  3. Approve each post individually — nothing is published without your explicit approval.
  4. The tool publishes approved posts at their scheduled time via TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Every post carries

  • TikTok's AI-generated content label on AI-made videos
  • Public visibility, with comments, Duet and Stitch enabled
  • Your caption and hashtags, exactly as you wrote them

Your data

The app stores your TikTok login tokens only to publish the posts you approve. Tokens are never sold, never shared, and never used for anything else. Disconnect the app anytime in TikTok's Settings > Security > Manage app permissions.
